Here is the lineup being tried out — personal AI agents you message like a person, that go and do things.

## Fo

Made by Wajo. You text, email or call it, and it handles research, writing, phone calls and everyday admin.

## Muse

Meta's personal agent. It works on its own sandbox computer, so it takes on longer projects.

## Tab

An assistant you text on iMessage or WhatsApp. It has its own phone number, computer and wallet for getting tasks done.

## Pally

A personal assistant that lives in your texts. It connects to things like email, calendar and files, and follows up on what you ask.

## Instinct

A personal assistant you text or call. It uses a phone and a computer the way a person would to take action across your apps.

## Hark

Hark Pro operates a computer for you and shows a small window of what it's doing as it works, with a focus on privacy.
